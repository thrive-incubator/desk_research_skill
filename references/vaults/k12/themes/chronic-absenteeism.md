## Current snapshot

Chronic absenteeism is the wellbeing-adjacent problem policymakers are most willing to act on, and
attendance metrics are the accountability frame through which wellbeing investments get argued. W32
added a **second route to the same outcome: litigation** — under an August 3 agreement settling an
October 2024 class action, **every New York City school must designate a school avoidance liaison
annually by October 31** and provide two hours of training on behaviors linked to school refusal, with
training materials due **January 1, 2027** and a compliance report by July 31, 2027; NYC may no longer
refuse special-education evaluations because a student is not attending.

W34 adds a small but rare signal of the third route: **a buyer naming attendance as a spending
priority.** Asked how it would use potential November referendum revenue, **KIPP Indy** — in line for
up to **$3.6 million** if Indianapolis-area ballot measures pass — said it plans to spend on
professional development and **"initiatives to increase student attendance,"** wanting to align the
money with "the strategies we believe will have the greatest impact on students." Modest in size, but
it is a named operator, a dated decision point (November 3), and an explicit attendance line — the kind
of observed purchasing intent the vault normally has to infer. See [[local-tax-referendums]].
*(as of 2026-W34)*

### 2026-W33 — Students miss 200M+ more school days a year than pre-COVID, with no agreed way to count it

### 2026-W32 — NYC must staff a school avoidance liaison at every school under a legal agreement
*Chalkbeat · August 3, 2026* · [link](https://www.chalkbeat.org/newyork/2026/08/03/nyc-lawsuit-agreement-creates-school-avoidance-guidance-liaisons/)
An agreement signed August 3 settles an October 2024 class action brought by the Legal Aid Society's
Education Law Project and Pillsbury Winthrop Shaw Pittman on behalf of four students with
disabilities who stopped attending school regularly. Every NYC school must designate a school
avoidance liaison — a teacher, administrator or other educator — annually by October 31, and provide
two hours of training on behaviors linked to school refusal, defined as missing extensive school
periods due to social or emotional disabilities like severe anxiety or depression. The Education
Department must create training materials by January 1, 2027 covering parent conversations and
gradual reintegration plans, and can no longer refuse special education evaluations because students
are not physically present. Compliance reporting is due July 31, 2027. No dollar amounts were
disclosed.

### 2026-W31 — Memphis moves all 69 attendance liaisons full-time amid state-takeover scrutiny
*Chalkbeat Tennessee · July 23, 2026* · [link](https://www.chalkbeat.org/tennessee/2026/07/23/memphis-schools-focuses-on-chronic-absenteeism-amid-state-takeover/)
Just over 30% of Memphis-Shelby County students were chronically absent in 2024-25 (18+ days)
against 18.3% statewide, though officials believe internal data shows the first improvement in
nearly a decade. All 69 attendance liaisons are now assigned full-time to the lowest-performing or
highest-absenteeism schools rather than split across buildings; the "Greatness At Your Door"
canvass in Orange Mound and Frayser enrolled 1,000+ previously unregistered students and lifted
attendance about 5 points in some areas. Staff distribute uniforms, transit vouchers, food-pantry
information, and mental-health referrals. Chronic absenteeism is one of four criteria triggering
the state takeover, temporarily blocked by a federal court in late June.
