## Current snapshot

**Food security is the student-support channel where the money is already appropriated and the failure
mode is uptake, not funding.** W34 opens the arc with an unusually clean case. Colorado distributed
**$57.6 million** in Summer EBT benefits last summer to more than **480,000 families** of
preschool-through-12th-grade students at **$120 per child** — and **35% of those students never used
the benefit**, sending nearly **$20.2 million back to the federal government**, enough to have fed more
than **168,000 additional children**. Colorado had the **lowest usage rate of the 20 states** tracked by
education policy consultant David Rubel; **Missouri distributed roughly the same $57 million and lost
only $3.5 million**, which establishes that the gap is operational rather than inherent.

The named causes are all logistics: a separate reusable card families lose or discard, low awareness
that automatic eligibility exists (students qualify automatically via SNAP, Medicaid, Colorado Works,
or free/reduced-price meal eligibility), **outdated mailing addresses drawn from prior-year district
records**, and general confusion about eligibility — plus some automatically enrolled families
expressing "hesitation or uncertainty about utilizing the benefit," per Colorado DHS. Families have
**122 days** to spend, with this year's clock expiring in late September. The state's remedy for 2026
was to draw addresses from the public benefits system rather than from school districts. Advocates
frame the stakes plainly: as Rubel put it, "the money was already allocated. Let's use it. We don't
have to fight over it."

Why this belongs in the vault: **benefit uptake is a distinct, badly-served problem from benefit
funding**, and districts hold the two assets that solve it — current family contact data and the trust
relationship. That makes uptake infrastructure a plausible district-adjacent product with an unusually
clean ROI argument, since no new appropriation has to be won. It is also the rare wellbeing spend where
the counterfactual is measurable to the dollar.
*(as of 2026-W34)*
