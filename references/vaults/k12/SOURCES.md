# Sources in the weekly K12 briefing

All free and public. Pulled weekly, covering the previous 7 days.

## News outlets (7 feeds)

- K-12 Dive
- Chalkbeat
- The 74
- EdSurge
- EdTech Insiders
- Hechinger Report
- Education Commission of the States

## News searches (11 standing queries)

- School-based mental health funding and grants
- SAMHSA / Dept. of Education youth mental health grants
- State Medicaid for school mental health
- State budgets and student mental health
- School district contracts and RFPs for mental health
- District budgets for counselors and therapists
- Charter schools and student mental health
- Charter school funding and grants
- Student mental health software, screening, telehealth for districts
- SEL platforms — funding and launches
- Studies, reports, and evaluations on student wellbeing

Higher-ed and non-US results are excluded automatically.

## Federal databases (2)

- **Federal Register** — federal rules, notices, and guidance. Searched on school mental health, student wellbeing, youth behavioral health, and Medicaid in schools, across the Dept. of Education, HHS, SAMHSA, and CMS.
- **Grants.gov** — open and upcoming federal funding opportunities on school mental health, student wellbeing, youth behavioral health, and SEL.

## Blocked, picked up second-hand through the news searches

Behavioral Health Business · National Alliance for Public Charter Schools · Brookings · Student Privacy Compass · ED.gov

## Not covered

- State allocation mechanics — state plan amendments, Medicaid policy bulletins, funding formulas
- District purchasing — RFPs, contract awards, school-board agendas (paid bid boards only)
- Peer-reviewed research journals
- Philanthropy and foundation grants
- State legislative bill tracking
- Practitioner voices (counselors, school psychologists)
