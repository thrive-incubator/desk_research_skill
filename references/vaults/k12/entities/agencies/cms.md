## Current snapshot

CMS (part of [[hhs]]) sets the payment rails that school-adjacent behavioral-health billing runs on.
Two actions define its current posture. It has proposed the CY 2027 Physician Fee Schedule rule
(published July 16, 2026) — the annual vehicle for Part B payment and coverage policy, including
telehealth and behavioral-health payment updates, whose benchmarks many state Medicaid programs key
off. And on **August 13, 2026** it issued a final rule prohibiting federal Medicaid and CHIP funding
for sex-rejecting procedures furnished to children. That rule addresses clinical procedures, not
school-delivered services, so the school-Medicaid billing channel is untouched on its face; it is
tracked here because it establishes that CMS is willing to carve categories of children's care out
of federal Medicaid and CHIP match by rule — and federal match is the mechanism the entire
school-Medicaid channel depends on.
*(as of 2026-W33)*
