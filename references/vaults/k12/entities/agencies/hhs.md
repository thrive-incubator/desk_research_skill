## Current snapshot

**HHS is now the primary federal actor reshaping youth-wellbeing delivery, and it is doing it through
program standards and grant terms rather than appropriations.** Three threads advanced in W34. On
**[[head-start]]**, the detail of the ACF proposed rule became clear: **more than 1,400 regulatory
provisions** are on the table across **1,600 grant recipients**, and the specific removals include
**the monthly mental health consultation requirement** and the fixed timelines for health checks
(insurance within 30 days of enrollment, vision and hearing screening within 45, immunization,
well-child and dental checks within 90). Teacher-to-student ratios go, with an estimated **80% of
currently enrolled children subject to worse ratios**; the expulsion prohibition goes; bilingual
instruction goes. ACF Assistant Secretary **Alex Adams** framed it as paperwork reduction and
flexibility on "personnel and workforce decisions"; Secretary **Robert F. Kennedy Jr.** said "it is
important to protect" Head Start and wants nutrition and physical-activity requirements added.
**Comment closes October 6, 2026.** On the **[[teen-pregnancy-prevention-program]]**, the
terminations reached the delivery layer with names attached: three New York grantees are losing over
**$4.3 million a year** serving over **19,000 young people**, and of 67 grants nationwide **all but
14 have been terminated** as the department shifts toward abstinence-based initiatives. On child
health, **CDC data released August 17** showed kindergarten vaccine exemptions rising to **4.2%**
(about 155,000 children, up from 3.6%), with coverage falling for every reported vaccine and
national measles cases at a 35-year high — against an **August 10 executive order** proposing to
narrow the childhood schedule from 18 diseases to 11 and split MMR into three separate visits. The
common mechanism across all three: rewrite the standard or the grant term, and the money redirects
without Congress.
*(as of 2026-W34)*

## Timeline

### 2026-W34 — Head Start rule detail: 1,400+ provisions on the table, monthly mental health consultation removed
*The 74 / The 19th · August 16, 2026* · [link](https://www.the74million.org/zero2eight/head-start-could-soon-be-overhauled-heres-exactly-what-would-change/)
The ACF proposed rule puts more than 1,400 regulatory provisions governing Head Start on the chopping block across 1,600 grant recipients, eliminating teacher-to-student ratios (an estimated 80% of enrolled children would face worse ratios), the group-size cap, the expulsion prohibition, bilingual instruction, and the fixed health-check timelines of 30, 45 and 90 days — and many mental health supports "such as the monthly mental health consultation, would go away." ACF Assistant Secretary Alex Adams framed it as reducing paperwork; comment closes October 6, 2026 (also: [K-12 Dive, August 19, 2026](https://www.k12dive.com/news/how-would-head-start-programs-fare-if-federal-standards-are-pared-down/828173/); [The 74, August 19, 2026](https://www.the74million.org/zero2eight/head-start-providers-describe-a-system-in-turmoil-amid-federal-cutbacks/)).

### 2026-W34 — Teen Pregnancy Prevention terminations land on three New York grantees, $4.3M a year
*Chalkbeat / New York Focus · August 14, 2026* · [link](https://www.chalkbeat.org/newyork/2026/08/14/trump-administration-cuts-sex-education-funding/)
Of 67 federal Teen Pregnancy Prevention grants nationwide, all but 14 have been terminated as HHS shifts toward abstinence-based initiatives. Three New York grantees are losing over $4.3 million in annual federal funding serving over 19,000 young people a year: Children's Aid ($936,700 annually, terminated effective immediately two years early, HHS citing lack of alignment with its priorities), NYC Teens Connection ($3.9 million over two years, 15 staff at risk), and HOPE Buffalo run by Cicatelli Associates ($1.4 million annually).

### 2026-W34 — CDC reports kindergarten vaccine exemptions at 4.2% as executive order proposes narrowing the schedule
*K-12 Dive · August 19, 2026* · [link](https://www.k12dive.com/news/kindergarten-vaccine-rates-continue-downward-trend-CDC/828160/)
CDC data released August 17 showed 4.2% of kindergartners entered 2025-26 exempt from one or more vaccines, about 155,000 children, up from 3.6%, with exemptions rising in 41 states and DC and 24 states above 5%; about 280,000 kindergartners lacked documentation of completing MMR, and national measles cases hit a 35-year high by July 30. President Trump signed an executive order August 10 calling for protection against 11 diseases rather than the 18 CDC recommended in 2024, splitting MMR into three single-disease shots administered at separate visits.

### 2026-W33 — ACF publishes the Head Start deregulation proposal; CMS narrows a child Medicaid/CHIP coverage category
*Federal Register (HHS, Administration for Children and Families) · August 7, 2026* · [link](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/07/2026-16134/reducing-federal-burden-for-head-start-programs)
The Administration for Children and Families published "Reducing Federal Burden for Head Start
Programs" as a proposed rule on August 7, 2026, paring back federal requirements on Head Start;
providers pushed back immediately
(also: [Hechinger Report, August 8, 2026](https://hechingerreport.org/it-wouldnt-be-head-start-anymore-providers-blast-federal-proposal-to-alter-preschool-program/)).
Six days later CMS issued a final rule prohibiting federal Medicaid and CHIP funding for
sex-rejecting procedures furnished to children
(also: [Federal Register, August 13, 2026](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/13/2026-16508/medicaid-program-prohibition-on-federal-medicaid-and-childrens-health-insurance-program-funding-for)).

### 2026-W32 — HHS cancels 53 of 66 Teen Pregnancy Prevention grants after rewriting requirements
*K-12 Dive · August 5, 2026* · [link](https://www.k12dive.com/news/trump-administration-targets-teen-pregnancy-prevention-grants/827001/)
HHS canceled 53 of 66 grants under the ~$101M/year Teen Pregnancy Prevention Program on June 26,
2026, three days after issuing new requirements mandating "abstinence-only sexual risk avoidance"
content and alignment with agency priorities barring "gender ideology" and DEI practices. The
program has reached over 1.4 million youth since 2010. A July lawsuit argues the changes violate the
program's statutory mandate to use evidence-based, medically accurate interventions.

### 2026-W32 — HHS proposes sweeping Head Start deregulation
*K-12 Dive · August 6, 2026* · [link](https://www.k12dive.com/news/HHS-proposes-deregulation-of-Head-Start-early-childhood-education/827179/)
HHS announced a proposed rule paring down federal Head Start program standards, framed as giving
states, local communities and families more autonomy and removing "duplicative regulations." Head
Start serves roughly 700,000 infants, toddlers and preschoolers from low-income families annually.
Formal Federal Register publication was scheduled for August 7, 2026
(also: [The 74, August 3](https://www.the74million.org/zero2eight/trump-overhaul-could-gut-head-start-standards-leaving-states-to-set-the-rules/)).

### 2026-W32 — HHS receives IDEA Parts B, C and D grant administration from ED
*K-12 Dive · August 4, 2026* · [link](https://www.k12dive.com/news/education-department-promotes-special-education-shakeup-OSEP-conference/826981/)
Under a June 2026 interagency agreement defended by Education Secretary Linda McMahon at the August
4 OSEP conference, ED's OSERS moved formula and discretionary grant program components for IDEA
Parts B, C and D to HHS, while ED retained legal responsibility for the education laws. Special
educators and disability-rights advocates warned the split creates confusion and reduces
accountability for compliance.
