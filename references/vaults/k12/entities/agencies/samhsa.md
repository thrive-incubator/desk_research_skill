## Current snapshot

SAMHSA (part of [[hhs]]) is an active federal funder of behavioral and mental-health services
relevant to schools. With the summer slate closed — [[trauma-informed-services-in-schools]]
(SM-26-006) on July 16, the mobile-crisis, early-diversion and tribal suicide-prevention NOFOs
mid-July, and the state integration (SM-26-021) and behavioral-health-PHI (TI-26-020) windows on
July 27 — the **three parallel CCBHC solicitations (SM-26-014/-015/-016) close August 17, 2026 and
are the only live opportunity**, now four days out. Their strategic weight peaked in W32: ED's
school mental-health grants survive only on a temporary restraining order running to August 24 (see
[[school-mental-health-funding]]), which leaves CCBHCs the most durable federal rail into
school-adjacent behavioral health — sitting at HHS rather than ED, and operating on Medicaid payment
rather than discretionary school grants. The parent agency's own posture is a caveat, not a
reassurance: [[hhs]] canceled 53 of 66 [[teen-pregnancy-prevention-program]] grants, moved the
[[head-start]] deregulation into formal rulemaking in W33, and had [[cms]] narrow a children's
Medicaid/CHIP coverage category by rule — so "durable" here means structurally sturdier, not
protected. After August 17 the SAMHSA board is empty, and nothing observed so far indicates what
opens next.
*(as of 2026-W33)*
