## Current snapshot

A **state-statutory school mental-health funding vehicle created by 2020 Iowa legislation**, now in
its **sixth consecutive year** of awards. For 2026-27 the Iowa Department of Education awarded more
than **$2.35 million to 10 districts** — Cedar Falls, George-Little Rock, Lewis Central, Oskaloosa,
South Tama County, Spirit Lake, Storm Lake, United, Van Buren County and Winterset — establishing
**19 new therapeutic classrooms** serving more than **215 pre-K–12 students** across 15 counties and
all seven of the state's Behavioral Health Districts. Therapeutic classrooms support students whose
emotional or behavioral needs affect their ability to succeed in the learning environment.

The dollar amounts are small, but the risk profile is the point: this is recurring, statutorily
created state money that has now funded six consecutive cohorts, which is the opposite of the
federal discretionary streams currently surviving on temporary restraining orders (see
[[school-mental-health-funding]]). It is also a **concrete, fundable unit** — a classroom with
staffing, not an abstract services budget — which makes it a repeatable model worth tracking as
other state legislatures look for school mental-health vehicles. Compare
[[florida-mental-health-assistance-allocation]] as the other state-level exemplar in this vault.
