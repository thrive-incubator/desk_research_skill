## Current snapshot

**A contracted provider of student mental health services — the clinical-capacity layer in an
observed district SEL stack.** Oceanside Unified School District in California struck a partnership
with The Stepping Stones Group to help provide and deliver student mental health services, alongside
[[move-this-world]] (Tier 1 curriculum) and [[riverside-insights]] (DESSA screening). Its position
is the structurally important one: at Foussat Language Academy the curriculum and assessment layers
exist to *generate referrals*, and a school with 700 students and one counselor cannot serve them
in-house — so a staffing/service vendor is what determines whether the referral pipeline ends in
treatment or a waitlist. This is the contracted-provider pattern the vault tracks under
[[special-education-services-demand]], appearing here on the SEL rather than the IDEA side.
*(as of 2026-W34)*
