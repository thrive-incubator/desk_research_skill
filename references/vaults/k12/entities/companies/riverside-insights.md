## Current snapshot

**Publisher of the DESSA, serving as the screening and triage layer in an observed district SEL
stack.** Oceanside Unified School District in California partnered with Riverside Insights for the
Devereux Student Strengths Assessment (DESSA) of social-emotional learning and student success.
At Foussat Language Academy, the assessment functions as the **early-identification mechanism** that
routes students to Tier 2 or Tier 3 support, and from the 2026-27 school year will also be used to
track progress in students' emotional-regulation skills over time. Teacher training on DESSA ran in
August 2026. Position in the stack: [[move-this-world]] supplies the Tier 1 classroom curriculum that
generates referrals, DESSA triages them, and [[stepping-stones-group]] absorbs the clinical load.
The progress-monitoring use is the commercially significant one — an instrument used for
longitudinal measurement is what an outcomes-based contract would be settled against.
*(as of 2026-W34)*
