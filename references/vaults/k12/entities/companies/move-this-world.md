## Current snapshot

**A Tier 1 SEL curriculum vendor, observed in an actual district deployment rather than inferred
from a budget line.** Oceanside Unified School District in California struck a partnership with Move
This World, and Foussat Language Academy — a 700-student K-5 public magnet with a single counselor —
piloted its curriculum from February 2026 as the whole-class Tier 1 layer of a multi-tiered system of
supports for SEL, launching fully in the 2026-27 school year with teacher training in August 2026.
The role is notable for how it is configured: the classroom lessons are described as doubling as a
**referral-generation mechanism**, alerting administrators, counselors and clinicians to students who
may need Tier 2 or Tier 3 support. In the same stack, [[riverside-insights]] supplies the DESSA
assessment for early identification and progress monitoring, and [[stepping-stones-group]] delivers
clinical services. This is one of very few instances where the vault has the vendor names rather
than only the district's budget action.
*(as of 2026-W34)*

## Timeline

### 2026-W34 — Named as the Tier 1 curriculum in an Oceanside, CA multi-tiered SEL deployment
*K-12 Dive · August 17, 2026* · [link](https://www.k12dive.com/news/how-a-california-school-is-streamlining-its-approach-to-sel-supports/827968/)
Foussat Language Academy (700 students, K-5, one counselor) in Oceanside Unified School District piloted a multi-tiered SEL support model from February 2026 using the Move This World curriculum for Tier 1, launching fully in 2026-27 with August teacher training. The district partnered with Move This World alongside Riverside Insights for the DESSA assessment and The Stepping Stones Group for mental health service delivery; the Tier 1 lessons also flag students for Tier 2 and Tier 3 referrals.
