## Current snapshot

Two service areas remain in transition. **Wilmington, Delaware** has no operating Head Start
program. **Lynchburg, Virginia** moved to interim management in late July 2026 after the local
nonprofit holding the grant lost its federal award (see [[lynchburg-head-start]]). In both cases
the trigger, the incoming operator, and whether the service area goes to open competition are not
directly observable — DRS decisions and monitoring outcomes are not published as a feed.

**The awards channel is now dark for a measurable reason.** USASpending's 93.600 data ends at
action date **August 19, 2026**, so the August 20-27 window contains **zero** award actions — this
is reporting lag, verified against the API across four windows, not a funding stop. Because no W34
run covered August 13-20, those actions were surfaced in the 2026-W35 digest as a catch-up:
**88 actions, net −$330,413**, with **$3,818,449** in positive obligations and six actions at or
above the $500k floor. The composition is the interesting part. The only large positives were
**Tribal AIAN continuations** — $2,317,468 to Pueblo of Isleta and $1,327,333 to the Confederated
Tribes of Warm Springs (see [[aian-head-start]]) — while four grantees posted negative funding
adjustments above $500k: Blue Water Community Action (−$951,731), Southlake/Tri-City Management
Corp. (−$936,076), Tri-County Community Action Agency (−$522,796, on an Early Head Start–Child Care
Partnership) and the Urban League of Metropolitan St. Louis (−$513,780).

Label those negatives accurately: all four are **action type D funding adjustments**, and mid-August
negatives of this size are normally grant-close bookkeeping on an expiring budget period, not
terminations or DRS consequences. They are a watch position rather than a finding — the test is
whether the same recipients post further negative revisions into the September FY close, which would
convert bookkeeping into a recompetition signal. That is now a third consecutive window with no
meaningful positive money movement outside the Tribal portfolio, which means grantee reaction to
the August 6 HSPPS rescission NPRM still will not surface in awards data until the FY2027 cycle
(see [[hspps-rescission-nprm]]). The rescission proposal would also streamline designation renewal
itself, dropping CLASS: Pre-K as the named DRS instrument and replacing the prescriptive
school-readiness-goal condition with "suitable results" — which changes what a transition is
triggered by. For vendors the operating lesson holds: a single-grantee account can change
counterparty mid-contract, so multi-grantee or regional-office-level relationships are the hedge.
*(as of 2026-W35)*
