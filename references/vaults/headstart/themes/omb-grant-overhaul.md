## Current snapshot

OMB's proposed government-wide grants rule (posted May 29, 2026 with 41 co-signing agencies, 100+
pages) would insert senior political appointees into merit review so awards "advance the
president's policy priorities," grant broad authority to cancel awards at any time without notice,
and bar federal funds from statistical or demographic analyses. The comment period closed July 13,
2026 with more than 278,000 comments — including a joint objection from 23 Democratic governors
plus Guam, and public opposition from Washington-state Head Start programs and officials. OMB
intends to adopt the final rule by October 1, 2026, now the watch date alongside the HSPPS final
rule (see [[hspps-workforce-rule]]). If finalized as drafted, every 93.600 award inherits
cancel-without-notice risk — founders should treat grantee budget stability as a live variable
(shorter pilot terms, milestone-based contracts) until the final cancellation provisions are known.
*(as of 2026-W29)*
