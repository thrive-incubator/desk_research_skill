## Current snapshot

The American Indian and Alaska Native (AIAN) Head Start portfolio is a structurally separate slice
of the program, and the vault tracks it as its own entity because federal actions keep treating it
separately. **ACF-OHS-IM-26-01 on enrollment reductions was addressed explicitly to "all non-Tribal
grant recipients,"** carving Tribal grantees out of the tightened Change in Scope review
(see [[enrollment-reduction-review]]) — so the sub-regulatory rules Tribal programs operate under
are already diverging from everyone else's.

**Award data marks it out too.** In the August 13-19, 2026 catch-up window, the only large positive
obligations on assistance listing 93.600 anywhere in the country were AIAN continuations:
**$2,317,468 to Pueblo of Isleta** and **$1,327,333 to the Confederated Tribes of the Warm Springs
Reservation of Oregon**, both action type B and both described as "AIAN Head Start and AIAN Early
Head Start." Together they were most of the **$3,818,449** in positive obligations in a window that
netted **−$330,413** overall (see [[grantee-transition-gaps]]). A $1,110,432 AIAN continuation to
the Shoshone-Bannock Tribes was the sole above-floor action in the July 30-August 6 window as well,
which makes this the second recorded instance of Tribal continuations flowing while the rest of the
awards channel was bookkeeping.

There is a parallel funding stream on the opportunity side: the **Tribal Maternal, Infant, and Early
Childhood Home Visiting Program** (HHS-2027-ACF-ECD-TH-0003) is forecast with an estimated open date
of July 17, 2026, separate from HRSA's main MIECHV competition (see [[hrsa]]).

Entry-strategy read: AIAN programs are a smaller but materially less crowded entry point than the
mainstream grantee population. They have their own carve-outs from OHS sub-regulatory guidance and a
separate home-visiting funding stream. The two named grantees above have fresh budget authority. The caution is that
Tribal procurement and governance are not the CAA or school-district motions a vendor may be
tooled for, and the relationship channel here is even less feed-shaped than the rest of the
sector. *(as of 2026-W35)*
