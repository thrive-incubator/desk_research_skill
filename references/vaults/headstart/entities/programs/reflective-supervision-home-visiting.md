## Current snapshot

An OPRE research funding opportunity on reflective supervision in home visiting — the IECMH
workforce-support mechanism, studied inside the MIECHV delivery system. Posted July 24, 2026, it
**closed August 24, 2026**, inside the 2026-W35 window; the application window is now shut and
awards are pending. Nothing in the 2026-W35 digest reports an award, so the outcome is not yet
observable.

With it closed, **no IECMH-relevant federal opportunity on the board carries a live deadline**:
every early-care opportunity currently listed is a HRSA or ACF forecast with no close date posted
(see [[hrsa]], [[aian-head-start]]). It closed as ACF proposes to remove the HSPPS monthly mental
health consultation requirement — federal money moved toward *studying* reflective supervision at
the same moment it stopped *mandating* consultation. That framing gained independent support this
week: a WAIMH study of an Irish paediatric hospital system identified structured reflective
supervision and shared organizational language, not curriculum, as the binding constraint on IECMH
practice (see [[iecmh-financing-shift]]). *(as of 2026-W35)*
