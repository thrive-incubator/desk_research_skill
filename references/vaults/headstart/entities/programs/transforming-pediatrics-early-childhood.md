## Current snapshot

TPEC is HRSA's vehicle for putting early childhood developmental expertise inside pediatric primary
care. Authorized by **42 U.S.C. 701(a)(2) — Title V, Sec. 501(a)(2) of the Social Security Act** as
a special project of regional and national significance, it "directly funds organizations with
statewide or tribal reach to place early childhood development experts in local pediatric practices
to deliver team-based care to young children and their families."

It is small — **10 funding recipients** — but structurally interesting: it is one of the few federal
programs financing the pediatric-integration route the IECMH field argues carries the leverage, at
the moment the HSPPS is proposing to drop its own monthly mental health consultation mandate.
Its **standardized annual performance measures went to OMB for review on August 10, 2026**, with
comments open to **September 9, 2026** (burden estimate: 10 respondents, 120 hours each, 1,200
annual hours). The prior 60-day notice in April 2026 drew no public comments. Those measures are
the definition of success TPEC grantees will report against — cheaper to map a product's outputs to
them before they lock than after (see [[hrsa]], [[iecmh-financing-shift]]). *(as of 2026-W33)*
