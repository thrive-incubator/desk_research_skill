## Current snapshot

A genuinely new Head Start market. ACF/OHS announced on August 12, 2026 its intent to award **two
sole-source grants totaling up to $7,200,000 — $3,600,000 each — to the Federated States of
Micronesia (FSM) and the Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI)** to support the establishment and
provision of Early Head Start and/or Head Start Preschool services. The authority is new: the
**Compacts of Free Association Amendments Act of 2024** (Division G, Title II of Public Law 118-42)
extended Head Start eligibility to the Freely Associated States, and FY2026 Labor-HHS
appropriations provided **$8 million** for it. The proposed period of performance is **60 months**,
subject to the availability of funds.

Entry-strategy read: these are two greenfield grantees building from zero on five-year money — no
legacy vendor stack, no incumbent student information system, no entrenched curriculum, which is
the rarest condition in this market. It is also small, remote, operationally hard, and sole-source,
meaning the recipient entity rather than a competition decides procurement. Worth a pipeline note,
not a pivot (see [[office-of-head-start]], [[acf]]). *(as of 2026-W33)*
