## Current snapshot

The Head Start program serving Lynchburg, Virginia is under interim management as of
2026-W32, after the local nonprofit that held the grant lost its federal award. The digest does
not name the outgoing nonprofit, the incoming interim operator, or the reason for the funding
loss, and DRS decisions are not published as a feed — so what triggered the loss and whether the
service area goes to open competition are not directly observable. This is the second
service-area transition the vault is tracking (see [[grantee-transition-gaps]]).
