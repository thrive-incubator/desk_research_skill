## Current snapshot

ACF is the HHS division whose regulatory docket carries Head Start rulemaking. It still has **two
live Head Start rulemakings running at once**: the May 11, 2026 workforce NPRM ("Restoring
Flexibility to Support Head Start Program Access"), whose final rule has still not appeared on the
docket, and the far larger NPRM from its Office of Head Start, *Reducing Federal Burden for Head
Start Programs* (RIN 0970-AD30, docket ACF-2026-0595), proposing to rescind and replace the entire
HSPPS — published August 7, 2026, with comments **closing October 6, 2026**. The docket's
deregulatory cadence is explicit in the rule titles themselves — "Reducing Bureaucracy and Burden
for Family Assistance Programs" (July 31) and "Reducing Bureaucracy and Burden in Family Violence
and Prevention Services" (August 3) — which makes the Head Start rescission one instance of a
docket-wide program rather than an isolated action, and raises the odds it is finalized in some
form.

The **TANF data-sharing routine use has now been delayed twice**. ACF first pushed the effective
date of subparagraph (a) under routine use 1 and new routine use 10 of Office of Family Assistance
system of records 09-80-0375 (TANF Data, published June 23, 2026 at 91 FR 37406) to September 1,
2026; a second notice published August 20, 2026 moved it again to **September 30, 2026**. Comments
closed August 11. Two consecutive delays on the same routine uses is a signal in itself: the
cross-program data-sharing channel that Head Start programs lean on for categorical eligibility
proof is being contested and rescheduled rather than settled, which matters to anyone building
eligibility, enrollment or case-coordination tooling that assumes ACF-side data flows between TANF,
CCDF and Head Start.

Also outstanding from the prior window: an OHS notice of intent to award **$7.2 million in
sole-source grants** to the Federated States of Micronesia and the Republic of the Marshall Islands
(see [[head-start-freely-associated-states]]). Beyond the docket, ACF's technical-assistance
publishing is a purchasing signal in its own right — it held up **Texas** as the practice case for
CCDF program integrity for other state lead agencies this week
(see [[hspps-rescission-nprm]], [[hspps-workforce-rule]], [[office-of-head-start]]).
*(as of 2026-W35)*
