## Current snapshot

HRSA is the HHS agency carrying the maternal and child health money that reaches Head Start
families from outside OHS — MIECHV home visiting, Title V special projects of regional and national
significance, and the MCH training programs. It remains the busiest funder on the vault's
opportunity board, and its position is **unchanged as of 2026-W35**: the same four forecasts sit
there with **still no close date posted** — MIECHV (HRSA-27-022), the Home Visiting Research,
Improvement, Sustainability and Evaluation Center (HV-RISE, HRSA-27-023), Maternal and Child Health
Encounters Networks (MCHEK, HRSA-27-038), and the MCH Nutrition Training Program (HRSA-27-036). A
fifth, ACF's separate **Tribal** MIECHV (HHS-2027-ACF-ECD-TH-0003), sits alongside them
(see [[aian-head-start]]).

Two reads for an entry-strategy audience. First, home visiting is comprehensive-services money that
enters Head Start's world from outside OHS, so it is **unaffected by the HSPPS rescission** — a
hedge against the rulebook risk (see [[hspps-rescission-nprm]]). Second, HV-RISE is a research,
improvement and evaluation center, which is the kind of vehicle a measurement or evaluation-tooling
partner subcontracts into rather than bids alone. The MCH Nutrition Training Program is worth noting
against the rescission NPRM's own stated rationale, which elevates "health, nutrition, and physical
exercise."

Its most entry-strategy-relevant vehicle remains **Transforming Pediatrics for Early Childhood
(TPEC)**, which places early childhood development experts inside local pediatric practices — the
pediatric integration route the IECMH field has argued carries the leverage. TPEC's standardized
annual performance measures are at OMB with comments open to September 9, 2026
(see [[transforming-pediatrics-early-childhood]], [[iecmh-financing-shift]]). *(as of 2026-W35)*
