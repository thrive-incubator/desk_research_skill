## Current snapshot

OMB is finalizing a government-wide overhaul of federal grantmaking (proposed May 29, 2026 with 41
co-signing agencies, 100+ pages) that would put senior political appointees into merit review so
awards "advance the president's policy priorities," grant broad authority to cancel awards at any
time without notice, and bar federal funds from statistical or demographic analyses. The comment
period closed July 13, 2026 with more than 278,000 comments; OMB intends to adopt the final rule
by October 1, 2026. Because Head Start money (93.600) flows directly from federal to local
grantees, the rule reaches the funding-stability assumptions behind every Head Start award (see
[[omb-grant-overhaul]]). *(as of 2026-W29)*
