## Current snapshot

OHS is the ACF office that sets and enforces the Head Start Program Performance Standards and
issues the sub-regulatory guidance (IMs and PIs) grantees actually operate against. It is running
two tracks at once, and they point in the same direction: **strip the compliance rulebook, defend
the slot count.**

On the regulatory track, its August 6, 2026 NPRM *Reducing Federal Burden for Head Start Programs*
(RIN 0970-AD30, docket ACF-2026-0595) proposes to rescind and replace the 2024 Performance
Standards across 45 CFR Parts 1301-1305 — striking more than 1,400 provisions, deferring group
size, ratios, background checks and transportation to state law, rescinding § 1302.33 child
screenings and assessments, dropping CLASS: Pre-K as the named DRS instrument, removing the monthly
mental health consultation requirement, and cutting the administrative cost cap from 15% to 5%,
against one new requirement of 30 minutes of physical activity per three and a half hours of
attendance. It published in the Federal Register on August 7, 2026; **comments close October 6,
2026** (see [[hspps-rescission-nprm]]).

On the sub-regulatory track, OHS issued **ACF-OHS-IM-26-01, *Enrollment Reductions*, on August 6,
2026**, its first IM the vault has recorded. Addressed to all non-Tribal grant recipients, it
establishes two review pathways for Change in Scope requests that cut funded enrollment: expedited
review with an intended timeline of no more than 30 days when the reduction is paired with a
proportional reduction in federal funding, and comprehensive review otherwise, requiring a
documented community needs case, waiting-list counts, per-slot federal funding compared to ASPE
state medians, and written community support from named stakeholders. The IM is signed by
**Lizbeth Casco White, Director, Office of Head Start** (see [[enrollment-reduction-review]]).
**No new IM or PI was issued or posted in the August 20-27 window or its lookback** — the
sub-regulatory channel has been quiet since IM-26-01, which is consistent with attention being
absorbed by the open comment period.

OHS is also standing up service in new territory: it announced intent to award **$7.2 million in
sole-source grants — $3.6 million each to the Federated States of Micronesia and the Republic of
the Marshall Islands** — to establish Early Head Start and Head Start Preschool services under the
Compacts of Free Association Amendments Act of 2024
(see [[head-start-freely-associated-states]]). Its Tribal (AIAN) portfolio, administered separately
and explicitly carved out of IM-26-01, is tracked at [[aian-head-start]]. *(as of 2026-W35)*

### 2026-W33 — Issues IM-26-01, tightening review of enrollment-reduction requests
*Information Memorandum · ACF-OHS-IM-26-01 · issued August 6, 2026* · [link](https://headstart.gov/policy/im/acf-ohs-im-26-01)
Addressed to all non-Tribal Head Start grant recipients, IM-26-01 establishes review standards for
Change in Scope requests that reduce funded enrollment under Section 640(g)(3) of the Head Start
Act. A reduction paired with a proportional reduction in federal funding gets expedited
consideration with "an intended timeline of no more than 30 days"; a reduction without one goes to
comprehensive review. All requests go through HSES at least 90 days before implementation with
SF-424, SF-424A, governing board and Policy Council approvals, and program and budget narratives;
comprehensive reviews add a cover sheet with current vs. proposed slots, budget, and per-slot
federal funding compared to ASPE-reported state medians, community child counts, the program's
waiting-list count, and an attestation of no new barriers for families, plus written support or
opposition from stakeholders including the Governor, Members of Congress, the Head Start
Collaboration Office director, and the state or Tribal CCDF administrator. Signed by Lizbeth Casco
White, Director, Office of Head Start.

### 2026-W33 — Announces $7.2M in sole-source awards to Micronesia and the Marshall Islands
*Federal Register · Notice of intent to award sole-source awards · August 12, 2026* · [link](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/12/2026-16392/announcement-of-the-intent-to-award-sole-source-awards-to-the-federated-states-of-micronesia-and-the)
ACF/OHS announced intent to award two sole-source grants totaling up to $7,200,000 — $3,600,000
each — to the Federated States of Micronesia and the Republic of the Marshall Islands to support
the establishment and provision of Early Head Start and/or Head Start Preschool services, under the
Compacts of Free Association Amendments Act of 2024 (Division G, Title II of Public Law 118-42) and
FY2026 Labor-HHS appropriations providing $8 million for the Freely Associated States. Proposed
period of performance: 60 months.

### 2026-W32 — Proposes to rescind and replace the entire HSPPS
*Federal Register · Proposed Rule · filed for public inspection August 6, 2026; publication August 7, 2026* · [link](https://www.federalregister.gov/public-inspection/2026-16134/reducing-federal-burden-for-head-start-programs)
NPRM *Reducing Federal Burden for Head Start Programs* (RIN 0970-AD30, docket ACF-2026-0595)
proposes to "rescind and replace" the Performance Standards last revised in 2024 across 45 CFR
Parts 1301, 1302, 1303, 1304 and 1305: more than 1,400 provisions rescinded, § 1302.33 child
screenings and assessments rescinded, CLASS: Pre-K removed as the named DRS instrument, monthly
mental health consultation removed, the administrative cost cap cut from 15% to 5%, and a new
requirement of 30 minutes of physical activity per three and a half hours of attendance. ACF's
RIA estimates $1,476,881,912 to $2,959,495,914 in annual quantified impact at full implementation
(primary estimate $2,218,188,913), ~$0.94 billion in annualized E.O. 14192 savings, and potential
reinvestment into 116,516 Head Start Preschool and 45,578 Early Head Start slots in 2031.
