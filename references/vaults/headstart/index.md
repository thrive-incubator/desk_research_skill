# Thrive Center Head Start Knowledge Vault

This vault is the Thrive Center Incubator's internal knowledge base for the **Head Start
ecosystem**, read through an **entry-strategy lens for founders and incubator staff building
products for that ecosystem**. It tracks how federal dollars and policy flow from HHS → ACF →
Office of Head Start directly to ~1,600 local grantees (no state pass-through), through grant
administration and awards, state and adjacent systems, and grantee operations, into the
technology/vendor layer and the ideas/research layer — so that staff can see what each week's
moves open or close for innovators. It is fed once a week by the `head-start-synthesis` skill,
whose synthesized digest is the raw material this vault accretes into durable, pre-synthesized
knowledge. The markdown files here are the source of truth (versioned in git) and are designed
to be navigated by an AI agent: read this file first, filter on front-matter, read entity/theme
pages for synthesized arcs, and read specific digests only when you need week-level detail.

## How to navigate

The vault has **two axes**:

1. **Chronological digests** (`digests/`) — the weekly synthesis output, append-only raw
   material. One file per ISO week (e.g. `digests/2026/2026-W29.md`). Read these for the detail
   of a specific week.
2. **Accreting entity & theme pages** (`entities/`, `themes/`) — the actual *knowledge*, built
   up over time so longitudinal questions ("what's the arc of the HSPPS workforce rule, and
   which portfolio companies are exposed?") can be answered from a pre-synthesized page instead
   of re-derived from scratch. Each page carries a mutable **Current snapshot** and an
   append-only **Timeline**.

Read order for an agent answering a question: **this file → relevant entity/theme page(s) for
the synthesized arc → specific digest(s) only for week-level detail.** Filter on front-matter
(`layers`, `entities`, `states`, `tags`) to narrow before reading bodies.

The full operating manual — schemas, naming, provenance rules, and the weekly update procedure —
is in [CONVENTIONS.md](CONVENTIONS.md). The controlled vocabulary (seven layers + canonical tags +
naming rules) is in [TAGS.md](TAGS.md).

## Active themes

Each theme links to its page in `themes/`.

- [HSPPS Rescission NPRM](themes/hspps-rescission-nprm.md) — OHS proposes to rescind and replace the entire HSPPS; **comments close October 6, 2026**. The vault's loudest watch item.
- [Enrollment Reductions & the Change in Scope Review](themes/enrollment-reduction-review.md) — IM-26-01 tightens review of requests to cut funded enrollment; 97,000+ slots eliminated FY22-FY24 is the government's own number.
- [HSPPS Workforce Rule](themes/hspps-workforce-rule.md) — the May 2026 workforce NPRM's final rule, still absent from the ACF docket; OHS now blames the 2024 rule for putting 100,000+ slots at risk.
- [IECMH — From HSPPS Mandate to Local Purchase](themes/iecmh-financing-shift.md) — the monthly mental health consultation mandate is proposed for removal; financing shifts toward Medicaid/EPSDT, pediatric integration, and named state and philanthropic commitments.
- [Community-Based Developmental Screening](themes/community-based-screening.md) — only 36% of parents complete a screener; screening moves to libraries and classrooms as § 1302.33 is proposed for rescission.
- [ECE Workforce Compensation & the Non-Wage Workaround](themes/ece-workforce-compensation.md) — pay is capped by the funding formula, so programs buy housing, benefits, financial coaching and child care for educator-parents instead.
- [Outcomes-Based Purchasing & the Evidence Bar](themes/outcomes-based-purchasing.md) — payment tied to demonstrated results is spreading in K-12; measurability becomes a product requirement, and the peer-reviewed literature becomes the counterparty's ammunition.
- [OMB Federal Grantmaking Overhaul](themes/omb-grant-overhaul.md) — cancel-anytime authority and politicized merit review, aimed at adoption by Oct 1.
- [Grantee Transitions & Service-Area Gaps](themes/grantee-transition-gaps.md) — Wilmington DE has no operating program, Lynchburg VA is under interim management; four grantees posted negative funding adjustments above $500k in mid-August.

## Tracked entities

Grouped by type; each entity links to its page in `entities/`.

### Companies

- [First Five Years Fund (FFYF)](entities/companies/ffyf.md) — bipartisan early-learning funding advocacy; publisher of the searchable section-by-section guide to the rescission NPRM and of the Ohio affordability poll.

### Agencies

- [Office of Head Start (OHS)](entities/agencies/office-of-head-start.md) — sets the HSPPS and issues IMs/PIs; author of the August 2026 rescission NPRM and of IM-26-01 on enrollment reductions.
- [Administration for Children and Families (ACF)](entities/agencies/acf.md) — the HHS division whose docket carries Head Start rulemaking; two live Head Start rules, and a TANF data-sharing routine use now delayed twice.
- [Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA)](entities/agencies/hrsa.md) — MIECHV, Title V SPRANS and MCH training; four forecasts on the board, none with a close date.
- [Office of Management and Budget (OMB)](entities/agencies/omb.md) — finalizing the government-wide grantmaking overhaul.

### Programs

- [AIAN Head Start](entities/programs/aian-head-start.md) — the Tribal portfolio: separately administered, carved out of IM-26-01, and the only source of large positive 93.600 obligations in mid-August.
- [Michigan PreK for All / Great Start Readiness Program](entities/programs/michigan-prek-for-all.md) — ~53,000 applications, up 16% year over year; the state-pre-K hedge against HSPPS risk, at 400% FPL eligibility.
- [Investing in America Child Care Partnership](entities/programs/investing-in-america-child-care-partnership.md) — Packard-backed coalition of CDFIs and county/city associations framing child care as economic development; money that never touches OHS.
- [Reflective Supervision in Home Visiting Research Awards](entities/programs/reflective-supervision-home-visiting.md) — OPRE NOFO; **closed Aug 24, 2026**, awards pending.
- [Head Start in the Freely Associated States (FSM / RMI)](entities/programs/head-start-freely-associated-states.md) — $7.2M sole-source to two greenfield grantees under the 2024 COFA Amendments Act.
- [SEED Act](entities/programs/seed-act.md) — $350 above-the-line deduction for early childhood educators; passed the Senate Aug 7, 2026, back to the House in September.
- [Transforming Pediatrics for Early Childhood (TPEC)](entities/programs/transforming-pediatrics-early-childhood.md) — HRSA program placing early childhood experts in pediatric practices; performance measures at OMB, comments close Sept 9, 2026.
- [Infant and Early Childhood Mental Health (SM-26-020)](entities/programs/infant-and-early-childhood-mental-health.md) — SAMHSA NOFO; closed July 16, 2026.
