## Federal Funding & Policy

### OMB grant-overhaul rule closes comments with 278,000+ filed; 23 governors object, Washington Head Start programs raise concerns
*Stateline · July 13, 2026* · [link](https://stateline.org/2026/07/13/democratic-governors-say-proposed-changes-to-federal-grants-would-harm-reproductive-healthcare/)

The proposed rule (posted May 29 with 41 co-signing agencies, 100+ pages) would put senior political appointees into merit review so awards "advance the president's policy priorities," grant broad authority to cancel awards at any time without notice, and bar federal funds from statistical or demographic analyses. The comment period closed July 13 with more than 278,000 comments; OMB intends to adopt the final rule by October 1. A coalition of 23 Democratic governors plus Guam filed a joint objection, and in Washington state the governor, maternity experts, and Head Start programs publicly opposed the changes (also: [KREM, Jul 15](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMi8AFBVV95cUxOTjhnNDVaN19IWE1BSUVZdmxjVTY0d0h6VG03ODlUNkNldkdfUURxZmNWaFNKUWhrcWZFMjBqbVlTTkYwVWV3OGtZYlFsTnE4UzY3MU5LOGN3WW1fbnhqWFM4VXRpS3E5NDRUa19KWDhDanBWMkRBWElSVlFkMnJYNkt5UWVWTF92RFlrM1pOa2dLSXRCNDd2WEZIUmRUd18yRDRTVmxyYmMyY2Q3S0NRNHlIbjdqU3QxMVVJTWZZSy1vMUlncDdGZDBrZF96MjZwckdYWnp1MVRYNjhqT2tnSHpyWXZLbWZER1cyOG1lejY?oc=5); [Seattle's Child, Jul 14](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMieEFVX3lxTFBJd0lhVTJXS0xtYTFYWG1hT1FITXMwRThRbGF3aVl1OXBMOWN2Tm5KS2diRk9Ib2hOZjJIa1l0d3lYVlZhLTUzVWt3RTY5YS00VFZmdlNQaG5hXzhHNHpOSWdPMkFQNS04b3dCLVd3SlRlLWhaamFWLQ?oc=5)).

**So what:** If finalized as drafted, every 93.600 award inherits cancel-without-notice risk. Founders should treat grantee budget stability as a live variable — shorter pilot terms, milestone-based contracts — until the final rule's cancellation provisions are known.
